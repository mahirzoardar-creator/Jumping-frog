What is Mercor?

Mercor is a marketplace that connects people with specialist knowledge (writers, coders, doctors, lawyers, finance people, teachers and more) to contract projects with AI companies. The work is usually reviewing, ranking or writing material so AI tools get better at their job.

How it works

  • You apply online and complete an interview and profile.
  • If you’re a fit, you can be matched to projects.
  • You’re a contractor, not an employee, so you handle your own tax.
  • You work remotely, usually on your own schedule.

The good bits

  • Remote and flexible
  • Pay rates are shown on roles
  • Uses your actual expertise

The not-so-good bits

  • Work isn’t guaranteed
  • Projects can start and stop quickly
  • Not everyone gets accepted

Who it suits

It’s a decent side income if you have solid professional or subject knowledge, are comfortable with online tests, and can cope with work that comes and goes. I wouldn’t rely on it as your only income.

How Mercor pay works

What to look at before you accept a project. I’m not quoting rates here because they change by project, so check each role.

What decides your rate

  • The project. Each role lists its own pay, and harder-to-fill specialist roles tend to pay more.
  • Your expertise. Niche qualifications and experience can open up better-paid work.
  • The contract type. Some roles are paid by the hour, others per task or deliverable, so compare them properly.

Questions to ask before accepting

  1. Is it hourly or per task?
  2. Is there a cap on hours?
  3. How long is the project expected to run?
  4. Is any training or onboarding paid?

Getting paid

  • You’ll normally need a bank account and identity verification. Check Mercor’s current list of supported countries and payment methods first.
  • Your first payment can take longer than later ones, so don’t count on it landing straight away.
  • Payment timings and rules can change, so confirm them on Mercor’s own help pages.

Tax in the UK

You’re generally treated as self-employed for this work. That usually means keeping records, and registering for Self Assessment with HMRC if your income goes over the thresholds. Check gov.uk or ask an accountant, because this isn’t tax advice.

How to prepare for the Mercor interview

The process can include an online profile and an automated interview. Here’s how to give yourself the best shot.

Before you start

  • Update your CV so your expertise is easy to see: job titles, qualifications, specific skills.
  • Pick a quiet spot with decent internet.
  • Test your camera and microphone.
  • Set aside uninterrupted time so you’re not rushed.

During the interview

  • Be specific. Back up your answers with real examples from your work or study.
  • Explain your thinking. Projects like these often value careful reasoning, not just the final answer.
  • Be honest. Only claim expertise you actually have.
  • Speak clearly and take your time. A short pause to think is fine.

If you don’t get picked straight away

That’s common. Projects open and close, and demand depends on the subjects being worked on. Keep your profile up to date, and check Mercor’s own guidance on whether and when you can reapply.

Quick questions

Can I use AI to answer the interview?

Check Mercor’s rules before you start. The aim is to show your own expertise, and breaking the rules could get you removed.

How long does it take?

It varies. Allow enough time to do it properly, and don’t rush.
